The entries are well sourced overall. Every quoted article, clause, amount and age I could open matched the official text. The real problems fall into three groups:

- Wrong process-cost sentence in two places. Mục 4 (consumer) and mục 46 (labour) reuse the generic court sentence. Consumer suits under 100 triệu go to the summary procedure with no court-fee advance. Most individual labour disputes need a labour mediator first.
- Missed amendments. ND 347/2026/NĐ-CP (amends ND 105/2025 and ND 106/2025) is missing from mục 6 and 26. TT 28/2026/TT-BNNMT (amends TT 75/2025) is missing from mục 25. None of these changes a claim yet. In mục 26, though, the licence-label claim is due to be repealed.
- Summaries and titles that say more than the body. In mục 25, the title sends depressed readers to 115. In mục 31, the summary hides that the spouse is told.
