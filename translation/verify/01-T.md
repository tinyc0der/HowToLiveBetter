Reviewer: independent bilingual check (zh → vi), 2026-11-02. Scope: the 33 entries marked `<!-- vi: T -->` in `work/vi/01/1.md` … `5.md`, each compared with the same number in `work/zh/01/`. I did not read `work/log/`.

## Patterns
- Vietnamese entitlement content added to T entries (mục 20, 21, 22, 30). All of it is sourced, dated and consistent with vn-baseline. The rule in TRANSLATION.md §1 (T = swap contact points and prices only) is not being applied. The lead should decide one policy: either relabel these entries L, or let T entries carry a one-line, baseline-sourced cost/coverage note.
- No systematic softening of hedges or negations. Numbers are faithful everywhere. Strength was lost in a few places: 绝大多数 became "phần lớn" (mục 32), 不能 became "không nên" (mục 32), and 要 became "nên" (mục 27 and 44, minor). One quoted text was strengthened: 时有发生 became "thường xuyên" (mục 33).
- Inconsistent terms across entries:
  - USPSTF: "Nhóm Đặc nhiệm" in mục 8 vs "Lực lượng Đặc nhiệm" in mục 18 and 39; bare acronym in mục 44.
  - Hip fracture: "gãy cổ xương đùi" in mục 13 vs "gãy xương hông" in mục 39 and 40.
  - Benefit level 中: "vừa" in mục 28 vs "trung bình" in mục 42.
  - The bacterium: "H. pylori" in mục 23 vs "HP" in mục 47.
  - "típ" in mục 8 and 18 vs "týp" in mục 22.
- 成 is rendered literally as "X phần mười" ("bốn phần mười", "sáu phần mười") throughout. It is understandable but bookish. Where Lợi ích has the percentage, "khoảng 40%" would read more naturally. I did not count it as an error.
- Outside scope, noted only: mục 5's Nguồn renders Chinese author names in Sino-Vietnamese ("Doãn Du, Lý Hải Giao"), which makes the citation hard to trace. Pinyin (Yin Yu, Li Haijiao) would be traceable. Some Nguồn fields (mục 30, 32, 33) use "; " as separator instead of " ; ".
